About Firstway Marine

A practical engineering partner for critical equipment

Based in Musaffah Industrial Area, M-40, Abu Dhabi, Firstway Marine Machines and Equipment Maintenance L.L.C. supports marine, offshore, oil & gas and industrial customers.

Our services cover the complete maintenance cycle — inspection and diagnosis, repair and overhaul, fabrication and machining, refurbishment, testing and spare-parts support.

  • Marine machinery and onboard technical support
  • Diesel engine, motor and generator services
  • Hydraulic repair, fabrication and precision machining
  • OEM and aftermarket spare-parts sourcing
